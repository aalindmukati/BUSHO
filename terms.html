Draft for review. This page describes the current synthetic demonstration only. It is not a production agreement.

Prototype use

The BUSHO demo is provided to show a reporting workflow using synthetic data. It is not a clinical system, an emergency service, or a source of medical advice.

Human responsibility

AI-extracted information is a draft. A worker must review and confirm a report before it becomes a verified operational record. Administrative requests remain drafts until an authorized person approves them.

Scope and limitations

The demo does not connect to government health systems, place orders, diagnose conditions, confirm outbreaks, or make treatment decisions.

Future pilot terms

Any real deployment would require approved terms covering accounts, service availability, security, data governance, support, liability, and applicable law.
